# SOARForge Support Model Draft

## Support channels
- Email support
- Optional Teams/Slack channel for enterprise customers
- Professional services for deployment and connector validation

## Severity
- P1: Platform unavailable
- P2: Export or validation workflow broken
- P3: UI/report issue
- P4: Feature request

## Suggested SLA
- P1: same business day
- P2: 1 business day
- P3: 3 business days
- P4: planned roadmap
